# Printer shows offline

## Symptoms

A shared floor printer shows 'offline' and jobs sit in the queue. Other users on the floor may be affected.

## Resolution

1. Check the printer's screen for paper jams, empty trays or error codes.
2. Power-cycle the printer and wait two minutes.
3. Remove and re-add the printer from the print portal on the laptop.
4. Clear stuck jobs from the queue.

## When to escalate

Escalate to End User Computing with the printer name and floor if it stays offline after a power cycle, or if several users on the floor are affected.
