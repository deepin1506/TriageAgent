# Swollen or overheating laptop battery

## Symptoms

The case bulges, the trackpad lifts or clicks on its own, the lid will not close flat, or the laptop is hot even when idle.

## Safety first

A swollen lithium battery is a fire risk. Stop using the laptop, shut it down, unplug the charger and do not charge it again. Keep it away from heat and do not press on the swelling.

## Resolution

End User Computing replaces the laptop or battery. Back up work from the cloud on another device; do not power the damaged laptop on to copy files.

## When to escalate

Treat as urgent because of the safety risk: escalate to End User Computing the same day and arrange a loan laptop.
