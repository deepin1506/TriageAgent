# How to raise a useful IT ticket

## What to include

A short title that names the problem, what you were trying to do, the exact error message, when it started, and how many people are affected.

## Urgency and impact

Say whether your work is fully blocked or there is a workaround, and whether one person, several people or a whole team is affected. This sets the ticket's priority.

## General questions

How-to questions and general requests go to the Service Desk, which routes anything specialised to the right team.
