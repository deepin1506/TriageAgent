# Teams call audio or camera not working

## Symptoms

Others cannot hear the user, the camera shows black, or audio is choppy in Teams calls.

## Resolution

1. In Teams settings, choose the correct microphone, speaker and camera.
2. Check the privacy settings allow apps to use the microphone and camera.
3. Close other apps that use the camera.
4. Update Teams and restart the laptop.

## When to escalate

Escalate to Application Support if a test call still fails. If only one meeting room fails, include the room name.
