# Reporting a phishing email

## Symptoms

An email asks the user to log in, confirm payroll or bank details, or open an unexpected attachment. The sender is external or looks like a lookalike domain.

## Immediate steps

1. Do not reply, click links or open attachments.
2. Use the 'Report phishing' button in Outlook.
3. If a link was clicked, disconnect from the network only if asked by Security Operations, and note what was entered.

## If credentials were entered

Change the password immediately from a trusted device and tell Security Operations. Treat it as a security incident with high urgency: attackers act within minutes.

## When to escalate

Any click on a phishing link, or details entered, goes to Security Operations straight away. Reports of the same email from several people help them block it faster.
