# Wi-Fi drops in meeting rooms

## Symptoms

Laptops lose Wi-Fi for a few seconds to a minute in specific meeting rooms, often during video calls. Other areas of the floor work normally.

## Likely cause

A faulty or overloaded wireless access point covering the room, or interference when many devices join the same access point.

## Resolution

1. Note the room name and the exact times of the drops.
2. Forget and re-join the corporate Wi-Fi network.
3. If available, use the wired port in the room for important calls.

## When to escalate

Escalate to Network Operations with the room name, floor and times. They can check the access point logs and replace faulty hardware.
